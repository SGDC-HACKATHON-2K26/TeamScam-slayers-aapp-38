## 🚀 About the Project

**Scam Slayers** aims to help people recognize potential online scams and make safer digital decisions. The project focuses on improving awareness of suspicious messages, fraudulent links, impersonation attempts, and common social-engineering techniques.

Developed by **Team Scam Slayers** for **SGDC HACKATHON 2K26**, the project explores how technology can make digital safety more accessible and understandable.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Online scams can trick people into sharing personal information, revealing credentials, clicking malicious links, or sending money to fraudsters. Users may struggle to distinguish legitimate communications from deceptive ones.

Scam Slayers aims to address this challenge through accessible scam-awareness tools and a user-friendly digital experience.

## 💡 Our Proposed Solution

- 🔎 **Suspicious Message Analysis:** Help users review messages for common scam indicators.
- 🔗 **Link Safety Awareness:** Encourage users to examine unfamiliar links before opening them.
- 🛡️ **Fraud Prevention Education:** Explain common scam patterns and warning signs.
- 🚨 **Risk Awareness:** Present understandable warnings when suspicious indicators are detected.
- 📚 **Digital Safety Guidance:** Promote safer online habits and responsible reporting.

*These are project objectives; actual capabilities depend on the implementation in this repository.*

## ✨ Key Objectives

1. Improve awareness of digital fraud and social engineering.
2. Make suspicious communications easier to evaluate.
3. Encourage safer handling of links, messages, and requests for sensitive information.
4. Provide a simple and accessible user experience.
5. Explore practical technology-based approaches to scam prevention.

## 🔐 Security and Privacy

- Avoid entering passwords, banking credentials, OTPs, or other sensitive information into untrusted tools.
- Treat scam-detection results as guidance, not a guarantee.
- Do not open suspicious links simply to test whether they are safe.
- Protect user-submitted messages and personal data.
- Use trusted reporting channels when fraud is suspected.
